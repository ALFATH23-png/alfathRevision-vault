# **2. SMART OBJECTIVES**

### **Objective 1 — Clarify Yuki’s Role & Expectations**
**Specific:** Conduct a formal role‑clarification meeting with Yuki to review responsibilities, behavioural expectations, and professional boundaries.  
**Measurable:** Completion of meeting + signed role‑clarification document.  
**Achievable:** Meeting scheduled within 5 working days.  
**Relevant:** Reduces confusion and prevents overstepping that triggers conflict.  
**Time‑bound:** Completed within **7 days**.

---

**Specific:** Implement conflict‑reduction strategies (mediation, communication protocols, feedback loops).  
**Measurable:** 60% reduction in reported conflict incidents (via weekly check‑ins).  
**Achievable:** Through structured interventions and monitoring.  
**Relevant:** Directly addresses team dissatisfaction and risk of resignations.  
**Time‑bound:** Achieved within **6 weeks**.

---

### **Objective 3 — Improve Yuki’s Behavioural Performance**
**Specific:** Provide coaching and behavioural expectations training.  
**Measurable:** 80% positive feedback from team in anonymous pulse survey.  
**Achievable:** With HR support and coaching resources.  
**Relevant:** Ensures sustainable behavioural change.  
**Time‑bound:** Within **8 weeks**.

---

### **Objective 4 — Strengthen Team Communication**
**Specific:** Introduce weekly structured communication sessions.  
**Measurable:** Attendance + reduction in miscommunication issues.

# **3. DETAILED ACTION PLAN (SITUATION‑LEVEL)**

---

## **A. Immediate Actions (0–7 days)**

- **Conduct a private meeting with Yuki** — address concerns professionally, using evidence not emotion.  
- **Clarify her facilitator role** — responsibilities, boundaries, communication expectations.  
- **Document behavioural concerns** — dates, examples, impact on team.  
- **Engage HR for support** — ensure process follows policy.  
- **Reassure team members** — maintain trust and prevent resignations.

**Outcome:** Yuki understands expectations; team sees action being taken.

---

## **B. Short‑Term Actions (1–4 weeks)**

- **Implement a behaviour improvement plan** (BIP)  
  - Communication tone  
  - Respectful collaboration  
  - Adherence to role boundaries  
- **Provide coaching or mentoring**  
- **Introduce weekly conflict‑free check‑ins**  
- **Set up anonymous feedback surveys**  
- **Monitor interactions involving Yuki**

**Outcome:** Early behavioural changes, reduced tension, improved communication.

---

## **C. Medium‑Term Actions (4–8 weeks)**

- **Facilitate mediation sessions** between Yuki and affected team members.  
- **Review progress against SMART objectives**  
- **Provide additional training** if needed (e.g., conflict management, emotional intelligence).  
- **Reassess team morale** through surveys and 1‑to‑1s.  
- **Adjust workload or responsibilities** if role misalignment persists.

**Outcome:** Sustainable behavioural improvement and restored team cohesion.

---

## **D. Long‑Term Actions (8–12 weeks)**

- **Evaluate whether Yuki meets expectations**  
- **Decide on next steps** — continuation, further support, or escalation.  
- **Embed new communication protocols** permanently.  
- **Strengthen team culture** through recognition, collaboration norms, and psychological safety initiatives.

**Outcome:** A stable, high‑performing team environment.
